# Paid-beta launch gates

The product advances by evidence, not by a calendar date.

## P0 before external beta

1. Clear the product name, seller identity, domain, App Store name, and trademark risk.
2. Freeze one permanent organization-owned bundle ID, App Group, and iCloud namespace.
3. Replace independent file writers with one transactional repository and revision model.
4. Preserve corrupt/unreadable data and stop sync until the customer chooses a recovery path.
5. Make CloudKit bootstrap and account transitions identity-safe.
6. Make photo asset conflicts and deletion explicit and recoverable.
7. Build first-run onboarding, local-only mode, sync review, calendar privacy, and launch-at-login consent.
8. Complete edit, reopen, delete, global Undo, and Recently Deleted on both platforms.
9. Keep external Codex execution out of the App Store target until a separately scoped helper is proven.
10. Finish sandboxing, icons, licenses, privacy manifests, export/erase, StoreKit recovery, signed CI, and production CloudKit tests.
11. Use synthetic preview data and privacy-safe diagnostics only.

## Alpha gate

- No known data loss or cross-account leakage.
- Onboarding completes in under three minutes.
- Signed upgrades retain calendar and startup preferences.
- Mac and iPhone recovery paths pass real-device tests.
- Accessibility paths cover keyboard, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast, and Reduce Transparency.

## External beta gate

- At least 60% of qualified testers activate.
- At least 40% return on day seven.
- At least 40% of activated interviewees would be very disappointed if the product disappeared.
- Online sync succeeds within 60 seconds at least 99% of the time.
- No P0 privacy, security, or integrity defect is open.

## Controlled paid-launch gate

- 50 paying customers before broad promotion.
- Week-four retention at least 25%.
- Refund rate below 5%.
- Crash-free sessions at least 99.8%.
- No data-integrity incident.
- Support load is sustainable.

Failure extends the current stage. It does not lower the gate.
