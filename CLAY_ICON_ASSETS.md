# Open Loops Clay Icon Assets

The icons were generated as individual transparent raster assets with the built-in Image Generation tool, then downsampled to 256 × 256 pixels for the native macOS bundle.

## Shared art direction

- Premium tactile polymer clay with subtle natural grain.
- Soft rounded geometry and a strong silhouette that survives reduction to 40 pixels.
- Cobalt blue for structure, terracotta orange for warmth, and warm off-white for framing.
- Front three-quarter view with soft studio light from the upper left.
- One centered object on a genuinely transparent background.
- No text, border, badge, watermark, glossy plastic, glassmorphism, or excess detail.

## Asset prompts

- `home.png`: A compact little house with a cobalt-blue roof, warm off-white body, and one terracotta-orange doorway.
- `loops.png`: Three slightly offset rounded task cards with cobalt-blue edges and one terracotta check dot on the front.
- `calendar.png`: A compact rounded calendar page with two cobalt-blue binding tabs and one terracotta date dot, without numerals.
- `settings.png`: A tactile rotary adjustment knob with a warm off-white body, cobalt-blue centre, and terracotta indicator notch; deliberately not a gear.
- `photo.png`: A compact rounded camera with a cobalt-blue body, warm off-white lens ring, and terracotta shutter button.

## Product use

- Home, Loops, and Calendar pair a small clay icon with a text label.
- Personalize uses the clay rotary knob.
- The empty photo state uses the clay camera.
- Close, completion, priority, and destructive task controls remain native system symbols so their meaning stays immediate.
- Settings lets the user switch between Clay and System icon styles.
